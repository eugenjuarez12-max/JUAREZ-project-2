Vision & Mission

Vision

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

Mission

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

  • Christ-centered learning
  • Quality and inclusive Catholic education
  • Innovative research and community engagement
The School Seal

The Emblem, Decoded

  1. The Twelve Waves
    The twelve waves at the rim represent the twelve apostles tasked by Christ to teach and evangelize.
  2. The Motto
    "Teach Ye All Nations" expresses KCC's response to Christ's call for evangelization and Christian citizenship.
  3. The Two Stars
    One symbolizes KCC's Catholic identity; the other represents its mission to spread knowledge, truth, and service.
  4. The Philippine Map
    The Negros-centered map represents the immediate community KCC aims to evangelize and the wider world it hopes to reach.
  5. Dark Blue & Gold
    Wisdom, integrity, and excellence: knowledge and moral strength, with gold as the eternal light of Christ.
Identity & Meaning

Core Values

K

Kindness

A KCCian upholds unity and empathy, embodying shared purpose and mutual support within the school and wider community.

C

Christ-Centeredness

A KCCian is grounded in living faith in God, guided by Gospel values that inspire words and actions.

C

Creativity

A KCCian embraces innovation through research and creative problem-solving in service of Church and society.

I

Integrity

A KCCian is committed to moral responsibility and academic honesty, exemplifying Gospel-rooted principles.

A

Accountability

A KCCian takes ownership of decisions and actions, contributing responsibly to society.

N

Nurturing

A KCCian cares for God's creation as a responsible steward entrusted to protect and sustain the world.

S

Synodality

A KCCian journeys together in communion, fostering collaboration and active participation in the mission of Church and community.
